Part III of V · modules 15–22

Physics & hardware

Eight modules on the physical system under every algorithm. Linear and nonlinear wave physics and harmonic imaging come first, then the transducer from the piezoelectric plate and its KLM model through matching layers, backing and lenses to CMUT, PMUT and new materials, array architectures from 1D to 2D and row-column, the front-end electronics and link budget, the simulation and characterization tools used to design and verify probes, and the acoustic output, bioeffects and regulatory measurements every transmit sequence must pass.
