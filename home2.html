Part II of V · modules 08–14

Ultrasound signal processing & estimation

Seven modules on turning RF and IQ samples into estimates. The analytic signal and IQ demodulation, digital filter design and matched filtering with pulse compression set up the signal chain; correlation-based time-delay estimation, estimation theory with the Cramér–Rao bound, inverse problems with regularization, and optimization with automatic differentiation then supply the tools that phase-screen estimation, speckle and bubble tracking, sound-speed tomography and differentiable beamforming are built from.
