Part V of V · modules 28–33

Imaging modes & capstones

Six modules that combine everything before them into imaging modes and research targets. Microbubble physics and contrast imaging supply the ULM signal source; Doppler and flow imaging and elastography are the established motion-based modes; and three capstones, ULM end to end, sound-speed estimation and aberration correction, take the course to the open research problems it was built for, each with its estimators, its limits and its literature.
