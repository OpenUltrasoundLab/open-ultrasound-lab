Part IV of V · modules 23–27

Image formation & quality

Five modules on how a beamformed image is formed and how its quality is measured. Beam patterns, k-space and coherent compounding give the aperture-to-PSF picture; Fourier-domain (f-k) beamforming and adaptive and coherence-based beamformers show the alternatives to delay-and-sum; speckle statistics define the image-quality metrics (CNR, gCNR, speckle size) used to judge them; and spectral estimation turns the same RF data into quantitative ultrasound: attenuation and backscatter.
