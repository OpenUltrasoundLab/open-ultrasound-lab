Part I of V · modules 01–07

Math & signal foundations

Seven modules that rebuild the mathematics every later module leans on. Complex numbers and phasors, the DFT and FFT done by hand, and convolution and LTI systems come first; sampling, windows and the aperture-beam Fourier pair follow; then linear algebra (matrices as operators, eigendecomposition and SVD) and random vectors with covariance and correlation. Each idea is anchored to channel data, IQ frames and beamforming, so by the end you can read the matrix, spectral and statistical notation of ULM, sound-speed and aberration papers without stopping.
